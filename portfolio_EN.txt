Arduino radar
Arduino prototype with an ultrasonic sensor mounted on a servo motor.
Development of a scanning radar built around an Arduino board, an ultrasonic sensor and a servo motor. The servo orients the sensor, which measures the distance to objects in different directions.
The measurements are sent to a computer to display a real-time representation of the radar. This prototype makes it possible to explore data acquisition, servo control and measurement visualization. Project not finished.

Vincent Rouet
Technologist and expert in electronic reliability and radiation
Experience: 19 years of experience
Company: ArianeGroup
Vincent is a component technologist (EEE and assembly) and an expert in radiation effects and electronics reliability at ArianeGroup SAS, where he also heads the technologists' department. His core mission is to ensure the reliability and quality of electronic equipment, guaranteeing optimal operation under the specific conditions of each mission.
His responsibilities span several key areas: defining technical requirements for materials and processes to prove the durability of electronic designs in mission environments; supporting design offices in managing embedded technologies; analyzing technological risks for electronic products and sub-assemblies, particularly COTS (commercial off-the-shelf) components, to assess risk and technology maturity; reviewing technical files from the design stage and analyzing technological choices against requirements; supporting the supply chain for subcontracted products throughout the product life cycle; inspecting boards and electronic sub-assemblies according to standards; carrying out technological and process surveys at subcontractors and analyzing each manufacturing step; contributing to anomaly resolution in production and operations; and participating in quality alerts and technology obsolescence notices.
Before joining ArianeGroup, he spent 14 years at Airbus Group Innovations as a research engineer in electronics reliability and head of the electronics assembly and integration reliability department. He also spent three years at VITEC MULTIMEDIA as an electronics and computer engineer in a design office.
What I find fascinating about his work is that it sits at the heart of space and aeronautics, where a single component failure can jeopardize an entire mission. His role combines deep technical expertise in radiation effects, reliability, and COTS risk analysis with a broad vision of the product life cycle, from design to supply chain and production. Rigor, curiosity, and a thorough understanding of materials and processes help ensure that equipment survives the extreme conditions of space.

Civic Engagement

Ingé+
Ingé+ is a mentoring program that this year brings together 20 INP students around a common mission: supporting BTS students in their path and in their questions about career guidance.
Specifically, each mentor talks with their mentees about once a month, remotely (WhatsApp group, video calls) or in person for the BTS students based in Toulouse, to get to know each other and share advice on subjects, engineering, study paths and companies. The strength of the program is its spirit of mutual support: everyone gives according to their availability, without needing to be an expert, because the essential thing is to be present and to listen. As a mentor, I see in INP+ a concrete form of civic engagement: giving my time, reassuring and guiding those who hesitate, and passing on the desire to continue their studies, in a spirit of solidarity and sharing between students.
