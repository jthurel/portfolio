Arduino radar

Arduino prototype with an ultrasonic sensor mounted on a servo motor.

Development of a scanning radar built around an Arduino board, an ultrasonic sensor and a servo motor. The servo orients the sensor, which measures the distance to objects in different directions.

The measurements are sent to a computer to display a real-time representation of the radar. This prototype makes it possible to explore data acquisition, servo control and measurement visualization. Project not finished.

TFA control

1/3

Transfer function analyser used to carry out the measurements.

Development of a Python program that controls a transfer function analyser (TFA) through a serial connection. The program automates the measurements and retrieves the data from the device.

This automation makes it easier to acquire repeated measurements and use them to study the frequency response of a system, notably as part of my Buck converter project.

Civic Engagement

Ingé+

Ingé+ is a mentoring program that brings together INP students around a common mission: supporting BTS students in their path and in their questions about career guidance.

Specifically, each mentor talks with their mentees about once a month, remotely (WhatsApp group, video calls) or in person for the BTS students based in Toulouse, to get to know each other and share advice on subjects, engineering, study paths and companies. The strength of the program is its spirit of mutual support: everyone gives according to their availability, without needing to be an expert, because the essential thing is to be present and to listen. As a mentor, I see in Ingé+ a concrete form of civic engagement: giving my time, reassuring and guiding those who hesitate, and passing on the desire to continue their studies, in a spirit of solidarity and sharing between students.
